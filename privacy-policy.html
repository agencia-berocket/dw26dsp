Registration and Account Information. When you sign up for an account, we ask you for your name, email address, the U.S. state in which you reside, phone number and password. Communications. If you contact us directly, we may receive additional information about you, such as your name, email address, the contents of a message or attachments that you may send to us, referral information, and other information you choose to provide.

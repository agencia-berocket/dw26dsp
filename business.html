What is a Business dWallet?

A Business dWallet is a data wallet that enables a business to own, save, and manage the value of its data. With a Business dWallet, companies can enrich their products and services with data, turning every SKU into a traceable digital record with product-level data ownership and richer context with every transaction. This enables businesses' customers to gain shared custody of their data and receive value, and it enables businesses to reach a targeted group of individuals in the marketplace. All of this comes with a secure way to certify, manage, and license data.

+

What does it mean to enrich a product with data?

It’s as easy as importing the product information and confirming the suggestions in DrumWave’s automated tool selected for the company’s data. The business has full control over the products uploaded into the ecosystem and can manage them at any time, making them available or hiding them.

Once the products are enriched with data, customers get the product as usual, plus the data that comes with it. With each transaction, the value of the data accumulates in their Data Savings Plan.

+

How does a business benefit?

A business gains on several fronts. Products enriched with certified data carry richer context in every transaction, and every SKU becomes a traceable digital record. The data a business already generates becomes a resource it can save, value, and license in the marketplace, creating a new revenue stream. And the gains go beyond revenue: by giving customers a share of the value their data creates, a business builds trust and stronger relationships.

+

How does a business earn revenue?

Businesses earn revenue as the data contributed is licensed for the cases authorized by the chosen Data Savings Plan. Data never leaves the system, so licensing happens as often as its value is used in offers and licensing deals.

+

How does this benefit a business’s customers?

Every purchase does more for the customers. Each transaction adds Data Certificates to their Data Savings Plan, and consumers receive cash benefits when choosing to license their data or accept an offer. Customers earn from the relationship they already have with the business, and that builds trust and gives them a reason to keep coming back.

+

What is an Enterprise Account?

Like banks in the financial system, Enterprise Accounts are authorized and audited businesses providing data monetization services within DrumWave’s Ecosystem. It’s an entity that issues dWallets and distributes and manages Data Savings Plans. An Enterprise Account provides individuals and organizations the infrastructure to participate in the New Data Economy at scale, supporting transactions that move money and data value simultaneously.

+

Does DrumWave sell my data?

No. Your data stays where it already is. DrumWave never holds your data, only the value it generates, like a bank that holds your money but does not own it.

Your Data Savings Plan holds Data Certificates that represent your data and its value. Nothing happens with them without your approval.

+

Is my data safe?

Yes. DrumWave protects your business data using robust industry-standard security protocols and encryption methods, ensuring your information never leaves our secure system. We maintain strict compliance with global data standards, including GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, and SOC 2.
